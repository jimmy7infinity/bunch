# Privacy Policy for Bunch

**Last Updated:** January 16, 2026

## Introduction

Bunch ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Chrome extension and services.

## Information We Collect

### Information You Provide
- **Twitter Account:** Username, profile picture, and Twitter ID (via OAuth)
- **Polymarket Wallet:** Wallet address (optional, for verification)
- **Profile Information:** Display name, bio, avatar (optional)
- **Messages:** Chat messages, reactions, and replies you send
- **User Activity:** Friend requests, blocks, reports

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Data:** Features you use, chats you join
- **Technical Data:** Browser type, extension version
- **Market Data:** Polymarket markets you view (for chat context)

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and improve our chat service
- Authenticate your identity
- Display your profile to other users
- Show market-specific chat rooms
- Detect and prevent abuse
- Respond to your support requests

## Data Sharing

We **DO NOT** sell your personal information.

We may share data with:
- **Polymarket:** To verify wallet ownership and fetch position data (only if you opt-in)
- **Twitter:** For authentication (OAuth)
- **Cloudinary:** For image hosting
- **Law Enforcement:** If required by law

## Data Security

We implement security measures including:
- Encrypted connections (HTTPS/WSS)
- JWT authentication
- Content moderation
- Rate limiting
- Banned user blocking

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your data
- Delete your account
- Opt-out of Polymarket verification
- Report inappropriate content
- Block other users

## Data Retention

- **Active Accounts:** Data retained while account is active
- **Deleted Accounts:** Data deleted within 30 days
- **Messages:** Soft-deleted messages removed after 90 days
- **Inactive Chats:** Deleted after 30 days of inactivity

## Children's Privacy

Bunch is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
