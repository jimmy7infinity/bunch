Method 1: Canva (Easiest - Free)

Time: 10-15 minutes | Cost: Free

Steps:

  1. Go to canva.com
  2. Click "Create a design"
  3. Select "Custom size"
  4. Enter 440 x 280 pixels
  5. Click "Create new design"
  6. Add elements:
    • Upload your logo (from frontend/public/logo.png)
    • Add text: "Bunch" (large, bold)
    • Add text: "Chat for Polymarket Traders" (smaller)
    • Add background color or gradient
  7. Click "Share" → "Download" → PNG
  8. Save as promo-tile-440x280.png
  9. Repeat for marquee: Create 1400 x 560 size

Canva Pro Tips

  • Use the "gradient" tool for modern backgrounds
  • Keep text large and readable
  • Use your brand colors (greens, purples from your UI)
  • Center align everything for best effect
  • Export as PNG (not JPG) for best quality

Method 2: Figma (Professional)

Time: 15 minutes | Cost: Free

Steps:

  1. Go to figma.com
  2. Create new file
  3. Press F to create frame
  4. Set frame size: 440 x 280
  5. Add rectangle for background (match your brand colors)
  6. Add your logo (drag and drop)
  7. Add text layers:
    • Title: "Bunch" (bold, 36-48px)
    • Subtitle: "Chat for Polymarket Traders" (16-20px)
  8. Export: Right-click frame → Export → PNG
  9. Save as promo-tile-440x280.png

Method 3: Photoshop or GIMP (Advanced)

Time: 20 minutes | Cost: Free (GIMP) or Photoshop subscription

Steps:

  1. Create new file: 440 x 280 pixels at 72 DPI
  2. Fill background with gradient or solid color
  3. Add logo layer (from frontend/public/logo.png)
  4. Add text layers with your content
  5. Apply styles (shadows, glows, etc.)
  6. Export as PNG: File → Export → PNG
  7. Save as promo-tile-440x280.png

🎨 Design Tips for Best Results

Do's:

  • ✅ Keep text large and readable (minimum 16px for small text)
  • ✅ Use high contrast (dark text on light background or vice versa)
  • ✅ Include your logo prominently
  • ✅ Match your brand colors from the app
  • ✅ Keep it simple and clean (less is more)
  • ✅ Export as PNG (not JPG) for best quality
  • ✅ Use your actual logo file from frontend/public/logo.png

Don'ts:

  • ❌ Don't make text too small (must be readable at small sizes)
  • ❌ Don't use low-resolution images
  • ❌ Don't include screenshots (save those for the 5 main screenshots)
  • ❌ Don't make it too busy or cluttered
  • ❌ Don't use copyrighted images or fonts
  • ❌ Don't include prices or promotional text
